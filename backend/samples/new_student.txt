Maya stepped off the bus and held the strap of her backpack a little tighter. It was her first day at a new school, and everything felt unfamiliar. The building looked bigger than her old school, and the hallways were full of students she had never met.

In homeroom, the teacher smiled and asked Maya to introduce herself. Maya's heart beat quickly. She gave a small smile and said, "Hi, I'm Maya." Then she looked down at her shoes.

After class, a girl with a kind smile walked up to her. "Hi, I'm Leah," she said. "I'm new here too. Want to sit together at lunch?" Maya felt a little more relaxed. Maybe this new school wouldn't be so scary after all.
