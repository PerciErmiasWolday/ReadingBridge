Maya stepped off the bus and held the strap of her backpack a little tighter. It was her first day at a new school, and everything felt unfamiliar. The building looked bigger than her old school, and the hallways were full of students she had never met. A bell rang somewhere above her head. Groups of friends walked past, talking and laughing about their weekend. Maya checked the room number on her timetable for the third time. Then she followed the signs to homeroom.

In homeroom, the teacher smiled and asked Maya to introduce herself. Maya's heart beat quickly. She gave a small smile and said, "Hi, I'm Maya." Then she looked down at her shoes. The teacher thanked her and pointed to an empty desk by the window. Maya sat down and lined up her pencils in a neat row. She listened carefully for the rest of the lesson, but she did not raise her hand once.

After class, a girl with a kind smile walked up to her. "Hi, I'm Leah," she said. "I'm new here too. Want to sit together at lunch?" Maya felt a little more relaxed. At lunch, they found two seats near the window. Leah told Maya which teachers were strict and which ones told funny stories. Maya laughed for the first time that day. Maybe this new school wouldn't be so scary after all.
